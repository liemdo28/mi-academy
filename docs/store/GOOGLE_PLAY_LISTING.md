## Short Description

Fun bilingual learning games for kids.

## Full Description

Mi Academy helps children practice early learning skills through playful, child-safe educational games.

Children can learn at their own pace with activities for letters, numbers, memory, logic, listening, and problem solving. The experience is designed to feel warm and encouraging, with friendly illustrated feedback, clear progress, and simple navigation.

Key features:

- Bilingual Vietnamese and English learning experience.
- Offline-first gameplay for child-facing screens.
- Parent-protected progress area.
- Gentle adaptive recommendations.
- No ads.
- No real-child photography.
- No social feeds or unsafe external links in child-facing screens.

Mi Academy is built for families who want a safe, friendly learning companion for children ages 5-12.
